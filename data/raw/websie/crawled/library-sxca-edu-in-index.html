About Us

The Fr. Valles Central Library at St. Xavier's College serves as the hub for academic and research activities on campus, fostering an environment that promotes learning and scholarly inquiry.

Equipped with the latest Koha library management software and advanced RFID technology, the library ensures seamless access to its vast resources.

With more than 72,000 print collections, the Fr. Valles Central Library provides students, faculty, and researchers with access to a diverse range of materials, supporting the academic curriculum and enhancing the overall educational experience.
